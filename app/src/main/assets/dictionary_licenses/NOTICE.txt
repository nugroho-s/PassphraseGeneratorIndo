This app's words_list.txt is a modified version of the upstream dictionary.
Modified on 2026-10-03 for Passphrase Generator Indo:
- Removed the Hunspell entry-count header and affix flags.
- Normalized Unicode to NFC, converted entries to lowercase using casefold,
  and removed duplicates.
- Kept alphabetic single words with lengths from 3 to 16 characters.
- Sorted the 42,816 remaining entries and wrote UTF-8, one word per line.
- Did not expand Hunspell affix rules into generated forms.
